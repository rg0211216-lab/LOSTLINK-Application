YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. Learn how LostLink handles your information while using our platform.

Last Updated: November 2026

01. Introduction

LostLink is a Campus Lost & Found Portal designed to help students report lost belongings and find items reported by other students.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the platform collects, how it is used and how it is handled.

02. Information We Collect

When you use LostLink, you may provide the following information:

  • Name and email address during registration.
  • College name and student ID.
  • Information about lost or found items.
  • Contact details and explanations submitted with claim requests.
  • Messages and feedback submitted through the Contact Us form.

03. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage student accounts.
  • Display lost and found item reports.
  • Help students communicate about their belongings.
  • Process and review claim requests.
  • Respond to messages and feedback.
  • Improve the platform's features and experience.

04. Data Storage

The current LostLink prototype uses browser storage technologies such as Local Storage and Session Storage to store certain information.

This means information may remain in the browser until it is cleared or removed. Clearing browser data may affect information stored by the application.

The current prototype does not provide a secure server-side database or automatic cloud backup.

05. Information Sharing

Some information submitted in item reports and claim requests may be visible to other students using the platform so they can identify belongings and review ownership claims.

Students should avoid sharing passwords, private identification documents or other sensitive information in public descriptions.

06. Data Security

We understand the importance of protecting personal information. However, this current project version is a frontend prototype and does not provide production-level data security.

Do not use real passwords or sensitive personal information while testing this prototype.

07. Your Responsibilities

  • Provide correct information when using the platform.
  • Keep your account details private.
  • Do not share another student's personal information without permission.
  • Use the platform only for genuine lost and found purposes.

08. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the LostLink team through our Contact Us page.
